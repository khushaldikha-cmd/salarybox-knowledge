## 1. About SalaryBox

SalaryBox is a staff/workforce management platform for businesses. It supports employee management, attendance, leave management, payroll, compliance, reports, employee self-service, live location tracking, field-force management, biometric attendance, and related HR operations.

Key capabilities include:

- Manage multiple company branches and departments
- Employee/staff directory and digital onboarding
- Attendance through GPS, selfie, QR, biometric, and kiosk modes
- GPS-verified attendance
- Geofencing
- Attendance regularisation
- Leave management
- Payroll processing
- Salary calculation
- TDS, PF, ESI and other payroll compliance workflows
- Payslip generation
- Attendance and payroll reports
- Live employee location tracking
- Field-force CRM
- Biometric attendance devices
- Employee self-service
- Notifications and announcements
- Documents and reimbursements
- Subscription and billing management

# 4. Trial, Billing and Subscription Rules

## Quarterly Trial

For quarterly plans:

- 7-day trial
- ₹1 verification charge
- The ₹1 charge is refundable
- Payment mandate is established during setup
- If the customer continues after the trial, the applicable quarterly amount is charged automatically
- If the customer cancels during the trial, the full quarterly amount is not charged

Source:
https://salarybox.in/resources/help-center/start-a-new-plan/

## Annual Plans

Annual plans are available through the web.

Source:
https://salarybox.in/resources/help-center/

## Upgrading

A customer can upgrade a plan at any time.

The upgrade can include:

- Increasing staff count
- Moving to a higher plan
- Adding features
- Adding add-ons

For a Starter-to-Business upgrade:

1. Log in to the SalaryBox web portal.
2. Open **Subscriptions & Billing**.
3. Select **Upgrade Plan**.
4. Change the current plan to Business.
5. Update user count if required.
6. Review the order summary.
7. Confirm the changes and complete payment.

The Business plan activates immediately after the upgrade.

## Increasing Staff Count

Staff count can be increased during an active subscription.

The additional user charge is calculated on a pro-rata basis for the remaining billing period.

## Cancellation

A subscription can be cancelled from **Subscriptions & Billing**.

Cancellation does not delete the company or employee data.

The subscription remains active through the applicable billed period.

# 5. Legacy Pricing

SalaryBox also has customers on legacy pricing.

Legacy plans were module-based and could include:

- SB Attendance Lite
- SB Attendance Pro
- SB Payroll Pro
- SB Locate
- SB CRM Lite

Important legacy-plan concepts:

- Legacy pricing is the older module-based subscription model.
- Existing legacy customers retain their applicable legacy plan unless they move to the new pricing model.
- The new Starter/Business pricing does not automatically replace an existing legacy subscription.
- Moving from legacy pricing to the new pricing model is a separate decision.
- Migration from legacy pricing to the new model is irreversible according to the current help-center documentation.

- AI-based attendance fraud prevention
- Configurable geofencing
- OTP/PIN login controls

## Attendance Reports

Detailed attendance reports can be generated from the web portal or mobile app.

Typical web workflow:

1. Open **Reports**.
2. Select **Attendance**.
3. Select **Detailed Attendance Report**.
4. Apply branch/department filters if required.
5. Select the month.
6. Generate the report.
7. Download it from Recent Reports.

Historical attendance can also be downloaded for a selected date range.

# 21. Useful User Questions and Answers

## What is SalaryBox?

SalaryBox is a workforce/staff management platform covering employee management, attendance, payroll, leave, reports, live location tracking, and related HR operations.

## How can I start a SalaryBox plan?

A new customer can select a plan from the SalaryBox web portal or supported mobile application. Quarterly plans offer a 7-day trial with a refundable ₹1 verification charge.

## How can I upgrade from Starter to Business?

Go to **Subscriptions & Billing → Upgrade Plan**, select Business, review the order summary, and complete payment.

## Can I increase the number of employees later?

Yes. Staff count can be increased during the subscription, with the additional charge calculated on a pro-rata basis for the remaining billing period.

## Can I cancel my subscription?

Yes. Subscription cancellation is available through **Subscriptions & Billing**.

## Does cancelling delete employee data?

No. Cancelling the subscription does not delete company or employee data.

## Can SalaryBox prevent fake GPS attendance?

SalaryBox provides fake-GPS detection/blocking as part of its attendance fraud-prevention capabilities.

## Can employees mark attendance using a phone?

Yes. SalaryBox supports smartphone attendance, including GPS, selfie, and QR-based methods.

## Can SalaryBox process payroll?

Yes. SalaryBox supports salary calculation, payroll processing, payslips, and several statutory compliance workflows.

## Can SalaryBox manage leave?

Yes. SalaryBox supports leave policies, balances, custom leave types, approvals, and leave reports.

## Can SalaryBox track employee location?

Yes. Live location tracking is available as a feature/add-on.

## Can I download attendance reports?

Yes. Detailed and historical attendance reports can be generated and downloaded.

## How do I contact SalaryBox support?

Email support@salarybox.in or raise a support ticket through SalaryBox.

1. Prefer the pricing page for current plan prices and feature comparisons.
2. Prefer the Help Center for exact product procedures and UI steps.
3. Prefer the Guide section for conceptual explanations and educational content.
4. Do not assume that a feature is included in every plan; check the pricing section.
5. Distinguish new pricing from legacy pricing.
6. For billing questions, check the current subscription/billing documentation.
7. For exact navigation steps, use the Help Center rather than inferring UI paths.
8. When information conflicts between older guide content and newer Help Center content, prefer the newer Help Center information.
9. Prices, plan features, UI navigation, and availability can change. Verify the official SalaryBox pages before giving time-sensitive pricing information.
10. Do not invent unsupported features, prices, policies, or workflows.
